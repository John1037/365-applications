Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the website at 365applications.com (the "Site"). The Site is an information page that directs visitors to the applications built by 365 Applications. Each application is a separate service with its own privacy policy, listed below.

What this Site collects

Nothing. The Site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. It does not load fonts, scripts or other resources from third parties.

Hosting

The Site is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse. This processing is carried out by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy; we do not use that information to identify or profile visitors.

Emailing us

If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list.
