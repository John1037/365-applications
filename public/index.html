About 365 Applications

We're an independent software studio building practical web apps for everyday problems. The name says it all: tools that earn a place in your routine every day of the year, not just on launch day.

This site is the front door. It doesn't run accounts or collect data — it simply points you to the apps, each of which is responsible for its own service.

  • FocusedEach app does one job well instead of trying to do everything.
  • EverydayBuilt for regular use, with the small details that make repeat visits painless.
  • RespectfulClear about what data each app uses and why. This site collects none.
